A Little About Me

A planner shaped by South Florida’s light, warmth, and love of gathering.

Born and raised in South Florida, I grew up around family celebrations that always felt bigger than themselves — long tables, salt air, laughter that outlasted the music. It taught me early that the very best weddings are the ones that feel true to the people at the center of them.

After earning my certification in event planning from Broward College, I founded Ashley Gonzalez Events in 2012 with a simple promise: to plan celebrations the way I’d want mine planned — with warmth, patience, and an unwavering eye for the details that matter.

More than a decade later, my team and I have had the honor of guiding hundreds of couples through their most meaningful day. Every celebration is different. Our standard is not.

The Studio Philosophy

Three principles that shape every celebration.

01

Design With Meaning

Every palette, every place-setting, every ribbon — nothing sits on your day by accident. We design intentionally so that the look of your wedding reflects the depth of your story.

02

Lead With Warmth

Planning should feel like a partnership, not a project. We move at your pace, listen more than we speak, and hold space for the vulnerability that comes with saying yes to forever.

03

Execute With Precision

Elegance is quiet, but it is not accidental. We build meticulous timelines, plan for the unseen, and lead your day so you can be fully present in it.

Beyond The Aisle

A little life off the clipboard.

When I’m not walking a bride through her ceremony order or troubleshooting a rain plan two hours before doors, you’ll usually find me somewhere near the water — slow coffee, ocean breeze, and a stack of design magazines.

I’m endlessly inspired by old-world architecture, printed menus, and the quiet ceremony of a well-set table. South Florida is home; the world is where we love to work.

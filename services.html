The Offerings

Services, thoughtfully shaped.

Whether you’re at the very beginning of dreaming or standing on the doorstep of your wedding week — there is a way for us to walk with you.

01  ·  Signature

Full-Service Planning & Design

Our most immersive offering — a start-to-finish partnership designed for couples who want the entire experience gracefully held from concept to send-off. We build your creative vision, curate your vendor team, own the timeline, and remain the steady thread woven through every planning conversation.

  • Complete creative direction & custom design boards
  • Venue sourcing & contract review
  • Vendor curation, negotiation & management
  • Budget architecture & monthly tracking
  • Guest logistics, room blocks & welcome experiences
  • Full-day production & on-site leadership
02  ·  Mid-Journey

Partial Planning

For couples who have made progress on their own and are ready for a seasoned hand to bring the vision to the finish. We refine what’s in place, fill the gaps, and step in as your creative and logistical partner through the final months.

  • Vendor review, referrals & missing-piece sourcing
  • Design refinement & styling direction
  • Timeline architecture & production planning
  • Rehearsal & wedding-day leadership
  • Vendor communication in the final 8 weeks
03  ·  Handoff

Month-of Coordination

A composed handoff for couples who’ve planned their own day and simply want someone to hold it all in the final weeks. We inherit your plans, master every last detail, and lead the entire wedding-day production so you can be fully present.

  • Full plan intake & audit
  • Vendor confirmation & final logistics
  • Detailed timeline & run-of-show
  • Rehearsal direction & ceremony coordination
  • On-site management for up to 12 hours on wedding day
04  ·  Away

Destination Weddings

From the Florida coast to overseas escapes — complete destination production with attentive travel logistics, hand-picked local partners, and a design language that honors the place. We travel where the story leads.

  • Destination scouting & site visits
  • Local vendor curation & multi-day itinerary design
  • Welcome events, rehearsal dinner & farewell brunch
  • Guest travel & accommodation coordination
  • On-location production for the entire wedding week
05  ·  Design

Design & Creative Direction

A stand-alone offering for couples who have logistics handled but need a designer’s eye to bring their aesthetic vision to life. We craft the complete visual world of your celebration — from florals to linens, tablescape to typography.

  • Full concept development & mood boards
  • Custom florals & installation direction
  • Tabletop, stationery & signage design
  • Lighting & ambience planning
  • Vendor coordination for design execution
06  ·  Beyond The Wedding

Engagements, Showers & Milestones

Weddings are the beginning of a longer story. We plan and design engagement parties, bridal showers, rehearsal dinners, and milestone celebrations for the families we’ve loved on — and the new ones we’re just meeting.

  • Engagement parties & bridal showers
  • Rehearsal dinners & welcome events
  • Anniversary celebrations & vow renewals
  • Corporate galas & large-scale event production
